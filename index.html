An independent house devoted to fiction that rewards the patient reader — books made slowly, with conviction, in the lineage of Joyce and O’Connor.

Our Mission

Silver Current Press exists to publish literary fiction with the patience and care it deserves. We work slowly, edit closely, and design our books to last. We honor the lineage of Joyce and O’Connor — writers who trusted their readers. We believe the finest books are made by hand, by people who love them, for readers who will keep them.

II · The House

Three Offices, One Press

The Editor’s Hand

Publish

We acquire fiction that earns its sentences. Each title is shepherded from manuscript to bound volume with deliberate care, never haste.

The Workshop

Edit

Our editors read closely, in the Maxwell Perkins tradition — querying with respect, cutting with reluctance, championing the writer’s own voice.

The Long Bench

Champion

We place our authors in the hands of readers who will sit with them — booksellers, librarians, and the unhurried critic who still believes in the form.
